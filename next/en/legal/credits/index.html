1.Stories

Stories written by StoryTime are labelled as StoryTime's.

A story written by an outside author is credited to that author and remains theirs; we use it with permission. To be filled in: Author's name as she wishes it shown, and the date of her written permission

2.Pictures, films and sounds

The pictures, films, characters and sounds are drawn and animated in computer code by StoryTime. We do not use AI tools that make pictures or videos.

There are no recorded voices yet. When there are, each voice will be credited here. To be filled in: Voices and their recording service

3.Fonts

The site downloads no fonts: it uses the fonts already on your device. To be filled in: If the brand adds fonts, each font and its licence will be listed here
