1.In short

  • We ask for little: an adult's email and password, and for each child only a first name or nickname and an age group.
  • No advertising, no selling of data, and no tracking or analytics tools.
  • We never ask for a phone number, a photo, a surname or a date of birth.
  • Your card number goes to the payment company, never to us.
  • You can download, correct and delete your data in Settings.

4.What we never collect

  • A child's surname, photo, voice, date of birth, school or location.
  • Your phone number, your contacts or your location.
  • Your card number or bank details.
  • Information from advertising networks or social media.

We do not show ads, we do not sell or rent data, and we do not build profiles of children or adults for marketing.

8.How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete a profile, its progress is deleted with it. When you delete the account, we delete the account, your email address in the account, every profile, all progress, your choices, your trial and your passes.

A few records stay after deletion, and only these:

  • Purchase and refund records, without your name, because tax law requires them. To be filled in: How long: period required by tax law
  • A trial record: a scrambled code made from your email address (a salted hash) and the day your trial started. It holds no email and no name, nobody can open it through the site, and it is used only to refuse a second free trial to the same address. To be filled in: How long: proposed 2 years, to be confirmed by our lawyer
  • The record of emails already sent to you (which email, in which language, when, and whether it arrived), without your address: in each record the address is replaced by the same kind of scrambled code, at once for earlier emails and as soon as the email confirming the deletion has gone out. Only if an email to you bounced or you marked one as spam does the address stay, on our do-not-send list. To be filled in: How long: to be set
  • A cancellation request you sent by the form. To be filled in: How long: to be set
  • A note that the account was deleted and when, without your email.

To be filled in: Backup copies kept by the database service, and for how long: to be confirmed

9.Your rights and how to use them

  • See and download your data: Settings, "Your data", "Download my data". The file holds your account, profiles, progress, choices, trial and purchases.
  • Correct it: change profiles, language and news in Settings. For anything else, write to us.
  • Delete it: delete a profile, or the whole account, in Settings.
  • Stop news emails: untick news in Settings, or use the link at the bottom of any news email.
  • For the email records or anything not in the file, write to To be filled in: privacy contact email address.

We answer requests To be filled in: within the time the law sets: to be confirmed by our lawyer.

10.Children

StoryTime is made for children, but only adults hold accounts and make every choice.

  • A parent creates each child profile. We ask only a first name or nickname, which can be any word, and an age group.
  • A teacher enters a class, never the children's names.
  • Children's screens carry no ads, no offers and no tracking. Buying, settings and anything about money sit behind a grown-up check.
  • The parent can see, change and delete each profile and its progress at any time.

To be filled in: Confirmation that a parent's consent covers a child's profile as described: to be confirmed by our lawyer

11.How we protect it

  • Every connection to the site is encrypted.
  • The database itself lets each account reach only its own data.
  • Passwords are stored only as a scrambled code.
  • Only the owner's account can open the owner's area, and changes to content, passes and refunds are logged.

If data is ever exposed, To be filled in: what we will do and whom we will tell: to be set with our lawyer.

12.News emails

We send news about new stories only if you ticked the news box. It is never ticked in advance, and you can untick it at any time in Settings or with the link in every news email.

Each news email is marked as an advertisement in its subject line, in Hebrew ("פרסומת") and in the email's language.

Service emails, such as confirmations, receipts and the reminder before a pass ends, are always sent and never carry an offer.
