2.What StoryTime is

StoryTime is an online library of stories for children aged 2 to 8. Each story has a picture book, a read-along mode, a short film and an activity to do together. The site works in Arabic, Hebrew, English and Danish.

Stories are grouped by age: 2 to 3, 4 to 5 and 6 to 8. We add stories over time, so the library grows.

At launch, passes are sold to customers in Israel only. The free trial works everywhere.

3.Accounts are for adults

Only an adult aged 18 or older may open an account. A parent or a kindergarten teacher opens one account and then adds profiles for the children who will use it.

  • A parent adds up to 5 child profiles. A profile holds only a first name or a nickname and an age group.
  • A teacher adds a class instead of children: a class name and an age group, never the children's names.
  • You are responsible for how the account is used, including by the children you let use it.
  • Keep your password to yourself. If you think someone else has it, change it in Settings and tell us.

4.The free trial

  • 3 stories from one age group, for 3 days, with every feature: book, read-along, film and activity.
  • No card is needed, and the trial never turns into a payment.
  • One trial per account and per email address, also after an account is deleted. To make this work we keep a scrambled record of the address; the privacy policy explains it.
  • When the 3 days end, the trial stories close. Profiles and progress stay.

5.Passes

A pass opens every story of one age group for 1, 3 or 12 months.

  • You pay once. A pass never renews by itself, and we never charge you again without a new purchase.
  • Access starts online right after payment, and the pass ends on the end date shown before you pay and in your purchase confirmation.
  • One account can hold passes for two age groups at the same time.
  • If you buy a pass while another pass for the same age group is still running, the new one starts when the running one ends.
  • We email you before a pass ends. That email gives the facts and a link to your account, never an offer.

7.Cancelling a purchase

You may cancel a pass within 14 days of buying it, or of receiving the purchase confirmation if that came later. We refund you within 14 days of your request. A cancellation fee of at most 5% of the price or 100 shekels, whichever is lower, may be kept.

The full rules, and every way to cancel, are on the refunds and cancellation page. To cancel now, go to Cancel a purchase.

8.When a pass or trial ends

The stories that the pass or trial opened close. Your account, the profiles and their reading progress stay, so you can pick up where you left off if you buy a new pass. Nothing is charged automatically.

9.Using StoryTime fairly

StoryTime is for your family, or for the class you teach. Please do not:

  • copy, record, download, print for others, publish or sell the stories, pictures, films or activities, except where the site itself offers it;
  • share your account or password with people outside your home or your class;
  • collect content from the site with automated tools, or try to get around the sign-in, the age gate or the access rules;
  • use the site to harm others or break the law.
