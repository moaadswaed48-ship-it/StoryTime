1.Our aim

We want every child and every adult to be able to use StoryTime. We build the site to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA, which Israeli Standard 5568 is based on. To be filled in: Version of Israeli Standard 5568 that applies: to be confirmed

2.What we have built so far

  • Phone first: every page works on a small screen, with large buttons and links.
  • Buttons and links are standard ones that work with a keyboard, and the one you are on is clearly outlined.
  • Nothing is hidden behind hovering with a mouse.
  • The words of each book are real text, so screen readers can read them, and the reader has a larger text size.
  • Films have captions in four languages, switched on from the start.
  • If your device asks for less motion, or cannot show 3D, the book becomes a flat book with no page-turn animation.
  • Arabic and Hebrew pages run right to left.
  • Screens for small children work by pictures, not words.

3.What is not there yet

We are honest about what is still missing:

  • The site has not yet been tested in Safari on iPhone and iPad.
  • There are no recorded voices yet; read-along uses test recordings.
  • A full check against the standard has not been done yet. We will do it before sales open.
  • In Arabic and Hebrew, a film's timeline still runs from left to right.
  • The site's final colours and fonts are still being designed; contrast will be checked again when they are set.
  • The payment page belongs to the payment company, which we have not chosen yet.
