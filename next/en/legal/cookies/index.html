Cookies and local storage

What the site keeps in your browser and why. No tracking cookies, no ads, nothing to accept.

1.In short

StoryTime uses no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. It keeps a few small items in your browser's own storage, only so that the site works: to keep you signed in and to remember settings. That is why there is no cookie banner to accept.

To be filled in: Whether the hosting service sets any cookie needed for security, once the site is live: to be confirmed at launch

2.What is kept and why

  • Sign-in (sb-…-auth-token)

    What it does
    Keeps you signed in on this device
    How long
    Until you sign out

  • Reading and film settings (storytime.prefs…)

    What it does
    Text size, captions, automatic page turn, shelf or list
    How long
    Until you clear the browser's storage

  • Open profile (storytime.activeLearner…)

    What it does
    Which child's profile was open last
    How long
    Until you clear the browser's storage

  • Film position (storytime.film…)

    What it does
    Where a film stopped, to continue from there
    How long
    Until the film ends, or you clear the storage

  • Grown-up check (storytime.gate.passedAt)

    What it does
    Remembers for 10 minutes that a grown-up answered the check
    How long
    Until the tab is closed

  • Email being confirmed (storytime.pendingEmail)

    What it does
    Shows your address on the "check your email" page
    How long
    Until the tab is closed

  • Small notices (storytime.access.dismissed, storytime.justDeleted)

    What it does
    Remembers that you closed a notice, or that an account was just deleted
    How long
    Until the tab is closed

3.While the site is in test mode

Before sales open, the site can run in a test mode, and a notice on the screen says so. In test mode, accounts, profiles and progress are kept only in this browser (storytime.local…), not on a server.

4.Other companies

No other company places cookies through StoryTime. When you pay, you go to the payment company's own page, which has its own cookie rules. To be filled in: Payment company and its cookie policy

5.Your control

You can delete everything the site keeps by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. You will then need to sign in again, and settings go back to their defaults.

6.If this changes

If we ever want to add an optional cookie or a measuring tool, we will ask you first with a clear choice, and update this page.
