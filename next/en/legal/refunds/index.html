Refunds and cancellation

How to cancel a pass within 14 days, how the refund works, and what happens to your access.

1.In short

  • You may cancel a pass within 14 days.
  • We refund you within 14 days of your request.
  • We may keep a fee of at most 5% of the price or 100 shekels, whichever is lower.
  • The free trial costs nothing, so it needs no cancelling. Passes never renew, so there is nothing to stop.

2.Your right to cancel

You may cancel a pass within 14 days of the day you bought it, or of the day you received the purchase confirmation, whichever is later. This holds even if you have already started using the pass.

3.How to cancel

Use whichever way suits you:

  1. Signed in: go to Cancel a purchase, or to your passes in Settings, and choose "Cancel this purchase". It takes one step after a grown-up check.
  2. Not signed in: fill in the form on the Cancel a purchase page with the email used for the purchase and the order number from your purchase confirmation.
  3. By email: To be filled in: email address for cancellations.
  4. By post: To be filled in: postal address in Israel.

To be filled in: By phone, if the law requires it: number to be added

We confirm every cancellation in writing, by email.

4.Your refund

  • We refund you within 14 days of receiving your request and cancel the charge with the payment company. To be filled in: How the refund reaches you (same card, other means): to be confirmed with the payment company
  • We may keep a cancellation fee of at most 5% of the price or 100 shekels, whichever is lower. To be filled in: Whether we keep a fee at all: owner's decision
  • You receive a credit document for the refund.

To be filled in: Whether any amount may be deducted for the days the pass was already used: to be confirmed by our lawyer. Until then we deduct nothing for use.

5.When the fault is ours

If you cancel because of a fault on our side, for example the stories do not open, the pass is not what we described, or access came late, we refund the full price within 14 days, with no fee.

6.A longer period for some customers

People with disabilities, people aged 65 or older and new immigrants (within 5 years of immigrating) may have up to 4 months to cancel, if the sale included a conversation with us. Our passes are sold online only, but if this applies to you, tell us when you cancel.

To be filled in: Exact conditions of this right: to be confirmed by our lawyer

7.After you cancel

  • Access opened by that pass ends at once.
  • Your account, profiles and reading progress stay.
  • Other passes on the account are not affected.

8.Nothing to stop

The free trial needs no card and never turns into a payment. Passes are paid once and never renew. There is no subscription to stop.

9.Deleting the account is not cancelling

Deleting your account does not refund a pass. If you want your money back within the 14 days, cancel the purchase first, then delete the account if you wish.
