Cancel a purchase

You may cancel a pass within 14 days of buying it, or of receiving the purchase confirmation if that came later.

  • The refund is made within 14 days of your request. A cancellation fee of at most 5% of the price or 100 shekels, whichever is lower, may be kept.
  • Access to that pass ends when you cancel. Profiles and progress stay.
  • The free trial needs no cancelling: nothing is charged, and it never turns into a payment.
  • Passes never renew, so there is nothing to stop.
