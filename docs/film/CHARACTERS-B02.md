# B02 "The Shell That Sings": character designs and sheet prompts

Age group 4–5. Cast from `content/stories/b02/cast.json` (hex values come from it; anything added is marked
**added**). Look: `STYLE-BIBLE.md`, light key B02 (rainy winter morning indoors). The sheet method (anchor, then
turnaround, then expressions, about 12 images per character) is described at the top of `CHARACTERS-A04.md`.

**Cultural neutrality:** no head coverings, no religious or national symbols, no writing anywhere in the room (no
alphabet posters, no name labels on hooks, no letters on toys). Children's skin tones and hair already span the
families of Israel and Denmark; keep them exactly as listed so no child reads as "the foreign one".

**This film has seven characters.** AI models mix up children in groups. Three rules: (1) each child has one
strong colour that no other child shares (listed as "signal colour"); (2) the five background children get full
sheets anyway, because they appear in the circle in almost every scene; (3) group shots start from a checked still.

## Dalia (`b02-dalia`), the heroine, about 4½

**Design idea:** small, careful and brave in a quiet way; her red boots and pink jumper find her in any crowd.

| Feature | Value |
|---|---|
| Height | the smallest child (cast 0.58); 4.5 heads; slim (girth 0.4) |
| Skin | `#d8a27a` light warm brown |
| Hair | long, straight, very dark brown `#2e1c16`, to the middle of her back, held off her face by a plain soft headband `#fff3e6` (**added**, keeps the face clear in every shot) |
| Eyes | large, dark brown `#3a2418` (0.88); soft thin brows `#2e1c16` |
| Top | knitted crew-neck jumper, soft coral pink `#f2a98f`, cream collar and cuffs `#fff3e6` |
| Bottom | slim trousers, denim blue `#5b6f9e` |
| Shoes | red rubber rain boots `#c8453a` (her **signal colour**: no other child wears red boots) |
| Prop | the shell, always in her RIGHT hand: see props |
| Acting | holds precious things close to her chest; speaks to the floor at first and lifts her chin slowly once she knows she is heard. Sad is small and soft: head down, shoulders a little hunched, never tears. |

```
DALIA: a small slim girl about four and a half, 4.5 heads tall, the smallest child in the group, light warm-brown skin (#d8a27a),
long straight very dark-brown hair (#2e1c16) to the middle of her back held off her face by a plain cream headband (#fff3e6),
large dark-brown eyes, soft thin brows, coral-pink knitted crew-neck jumper (#f2a98f) with cream collar and cuffs,
slim denim-blue trousers (#5b6f9e), shiny red rubber rain boots (#c8453a). No jewellery, no print or writing on clothes.
```
- Pose: standing, right hand closed in a small fist held against her chest.
- Turnaround extra: "Right hand closed in a soft fist against her chest in every view."
- Expressions: (1) calm neutral; (2) secret little smile looking down at her closed hand; (3) shy hopeful smile, chin down;
  (4) small sad look down, brows tilted, lips pressed, no tears; (5) whispering with eyes half closed; (6) listening,
  head tilted, eyes soft; (7) sitting up proud, chin lifted, warm smile; (8) laughing a little, hand near mouth;
  (9) wonder, eyes wide, small "o" mouth.

```
DRUM BOY: a sturdy cheerful boy about five, 4.5 heads tall, deep-brown skin (#7a4a30), short close black-brown hair (#1c1210),
```

- Turnaround extra: "Seated in the same wheelchair in every view; both rear wheels round and identical; five spokes each."

- Expressions: (1) calm neutral; (2) huge grin, drumsticks raised; (3) bouncing laugh, eyes squeezed;

  an "O" of amazement (listening to the shell); (7) whispering with delight; (8) eager nod, quiet voice;
  (9) soft smile, tapping a finger.
