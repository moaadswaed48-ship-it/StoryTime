# C01 "Ten Pomegranates, Two Baskets": character designs and sheet prompts

Age group 6–8. Cast from `content/stories/c01/cast.json` (hex values come from it; anything added is marked
**added**). Look: `STYLE-BIBLE.md`, light key C01 (late autumn afternoon: the closest of our films to the
reference's golden light). The sheet method (anchor, turnaround, expressions; about 12 images per character) is at
the top of `CHARACTERS-A04.md`; the fill-in templates are at the top of `CHARACTERS-B02.md`.

**Cultural neutrality:** an open-air market that could be in any Mediterranean town: no landmarks, no signs, no
writing on crates, sacks, awnings or the coin tin, no flags. The seller's headscarf is worn the way market women of
many backgrounds wear one (tied at the back of the neck), with no symbol. Nobody wears religious items.

**This is a counting story.** The exact numbers (10; 5 and 5; 7 and 3; 8 and 2) are the lesson. Models cannot be
trusted to keep counts, so every count shot starts from a checked still and moving fruit is added by us in
assembly (see `SHOTS-C01.md` and `STYLE-BIBLE.md` section 13).

  with both hands; (8) wonder at the glowing seeds; (9) tired happy smile.
