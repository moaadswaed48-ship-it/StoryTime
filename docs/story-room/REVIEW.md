## 10. The three pilot stories to make first

Together they cover hills, a kindergarten and a market; spring, winter and autumn; calm wonder, quiet feeling and busy
comedy; a grandfather and boy, a girl with her class (including a boy in a wheelchair), and two children with three
adult traders; and three of the owner's named lessons. Each needs few puppets and simple backgrounds.

**A04 · Slow, Slow, Together** (ages 2 to 3, respect for the elderly).
Two characters, rolling hills and a field of anemones made from one repeated flower shape; the film's two speeds and the
small creatures that appear when the camera slows are exactly what code animation does well.

**B02 · The Shell That Sings** (ages 4 to 5, respect for classmates).
One room, six children in a circle and rain on a window; the story is carried by sound fading child by child, which
tests the film's sound design and puppet acting without demanding complex scenery, and it shows a disabled child leading.

**C01 · Ten Pomegranates, Two Baskets** (ages 6 to 8, numbers).
Round fruit, two baskets and five characters at one stall: simple shapes in lively motion, a skill story beside the two
value stories, and the clearest test of how the films teach maths with no digits in the picture.
