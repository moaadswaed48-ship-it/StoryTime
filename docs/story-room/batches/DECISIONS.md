# Conductor decisions on writers' questions (25 Nov 2026)

Apply these in the language-review pass of each batch.

- **B13 tile colours:** "blue and white" reads as the Israeli flag in Hebrew (and is noticed by Arab families too).
  Change the tiles to **yellow and green** in all four languages, including the English title
  ("The Yellow-Green Path"), the art notes and the film notes. Hebrew and Arabic keep a neutral title about the
  tiled path. Our rule: no political or national symbols in any story.

- **B10 "babies sleep on their backs":** this is correct and current safe-sleep advice. Source: American Academy of
  Pediatrics, "Sleep-Related Infant Deaths: Updated 2022 Recommendations", Pediatrics 2022;150(1):e2022057990
  (back to sleep for every sleep). Mark it verified with that source in the batch notes.
- **Background characters added by writers** (B11 a boy and Tala's mother, B15 a classmate): accepted; add them to the
  name ledger with names in four languages.
- **A-group sound word "Wheee" (A1 batch):** the Arabic became وي, which can read as "woe". Restore a joyful stretched
  sound once the check tool accepts stretched sound words (tool fix pending).
