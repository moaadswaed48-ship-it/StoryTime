  repair comes from the child who groaned. Danish names the round *Sneglerunden* (the snail round).
- **C03:** the low point is the little sister reaching for Mum's door to say goodbye; the note is Emil's answer. The

- **C04:** Leo tells the cousin's riddle ("What has a neck but no head?") and, when Grandpa mishears, shows the word
  with his hands. Each language has its own mishearing: deck (en), رُكْبَة for رَقَبَة (ar), צַבָּר for צַוָּאר (he),
  laks for hals (da). Grandpa's shepherd joke: his singing was so bad the thirty goats ran home.
- **C05:** the classmate who mutters "They're ours" is the one who hands Julia the last half ("You forgot yourself").
  The 22 halves include Julia's own, so the sum is checked on the page.
- **C06:** the low point is a squeak at the hall rehearsal on day twenty; nobody laughs. Grandma's song is unnamed.
- **C07:** the outline ends at the school gate. I added **one extra morning** (pages 12 to 14) in which Noah leads,
  looks at the quiet crossing, sees the same electric bicycle in good time, and the rider stops and waves them
  across. It repairs the rider and shows the rule used. If the outline must end at the gate, pages 12 to 14 and
  shots 30 to 38 need rewriting.

  finding in the brief. No beach flags anywhere.
