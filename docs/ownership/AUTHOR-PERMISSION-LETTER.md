# Author's permission letter: draft for the lawyer

**DRAFT FOR THE LAWYER. Not legal advice. Do not send to the author before a lawyer has read and corrected it.**
Prepared 25 Nov 2026. The owner, not StoryTime's AI sessions, deals with the author (project rule 3: "Never contact
her").

**Why it is needed:** the author's story is hers. StoryTime may not publish, record, add vowel marks to, translate,
illustrate or animate it without her written consent (project rule 3; `docs/LEGAL-CHECKLIST.md` item 37: the law
requires a written document for an assignment or an exclusive licence). Her book stays Arabic-only and unmarked
until she signs (`docs/MASTER-PLAN.md` §3.3).

**Notes for the lawyer before the text:**
- **Assignable?** The site is to be sold. Clause 6 lets StoryTime pass the permission to a buyer. Without it, the
  buyer must get her own letter. Please decide the wording (`WHAT-IS-TRANSFERRED.md` Q11).
- **Her approval of changes.** Clauses 3 and 4 give her the right to read and approve vowel marks and translations
  before publication. This protects her moral rights (credit and integrity, which stay with her; `israel-law-raw.md`
  §7) and matches the project rule.
- **An English draft translation already exists** in the project (`content/author/autumn-story.ts`), written by
  StoryTime without her consent and never published. Clause 4 lets her approve or refuse it.
- **Exclusive or not:** the draft is **non-exclusive** (she may still publish her story elsewhere). Change if agreed.
- **Which language prevails** if the three versions differ: blank, for the lawyer.
- Blanks are shown as `[____]`.
