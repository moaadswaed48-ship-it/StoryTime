# Wigglewild: brief (draft)

**The idea.** A living island where you play as a creature of your own making. Your body decides what you can do: each part you give it brings an ability.

**What it's going for.**

- Creatures that feel alive and silly: no two bodies are alike, yet every one walks on its own legs, with planted feet, swinging tails and wobbling eyes.
- Two ways through the island: make friends through short performances on a beat, or take the predator's path and fight.
- A world that goes on without you: species with their own temperaments and nests, day and night, and a giant that roams.
- Making as play: build in the Editor, study in the Lab, breed in the Meadow, then walk around as the result.

**What it isn't.** A game of models and animation files. Everything you see is grown in code as it loads.
